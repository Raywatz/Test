# Geometry + Algebra 2 Self-Study Plan (Common Core)

This plan covers every Common Core standard for **Geometry** and **Algebra 2**: the content in 2019-era Common Core textbooks (Pearson/Savvas enVision, Big Ideas Math, McGraw-Hill, Houghton Mifflin). The units follow the usual chapter order, so they line up roughly with your book's chapters.

Files:
1. `00-START-HERE.md`: this page (how to study, schedule, prerequisite check)
2. `01-geometry.md`: 12 units, about 55 lessons
3. `02-algebra-2.md`: 8 units plus extras, about 50 lessons

## How to use each lesson

Every lesson has the same parts:
- **Goal**: what you should be able to do afterward.
- **Learn**: the definitions, theorems and formulas.
- **Example**: a worked problem. Cover the solution and try it yourself first.
- **Practice**: problems with answers at the end of each unit.

A good study loop for one lesson (about 45–60 min):
1. Read **Learn**, then copy the formulas into your own notebook by hand.
2. Do the **Example** without looking, then compare.
3. Do the **Practice**. Check answers. Redo anything you missed the next day.
4. Do 10–20 extra problems on the same topic from your textbook (the odd-numbered ones usually have answers in the back).
5. At the end of each unit, take the textbook's chapter test closed-book.

## Prerequisite check (Algebra 1)

You'll use these constantly. If you miss any, review that topic first (Khan Academy's Algebra 1 course is free and good for this).

1. Solve: 3(x − 4) + 2 = 5x + 6
2. Slope of the line through (2, −1) and (6, 7); then write its equation.
3. Factor: x² − 5x − 14
4. Factor: 4x² − 49
5. Simplify: √72
6. Solve: x² = 50
7. Solve the system: 2x + y = 7 and x − y = 2
8. Simplify: (3x²y)(−2xy³)
9. Solve: |x − 3| = 5
10. Solve: 2x − 5 < 9

**Answers:** 1) x = −8 2) m = 2, y = 2x − 5 3) (x − 7)(x + 2) 4) (2x − 7)(2x + 7) 5) 6√2 6) x = ±5√2 7) (3, 1) 8) −6x³y⁴ 9) x = 8 or −2 10) x < 7
